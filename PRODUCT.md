# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Audience:** Movie lovers, anime fans, binge-watchers, and homelab self-hosters running AetherFlix on personal devices, Docker servers, laptops, and living room Smart TVs (Android TV, Samsung Tizen).
- **Context:** Often used in dimly lit or dark rooms (living room couch 10-foot viewing, late-night desktop browsing, or tablet/mobile streaming in bed).
- **Job to be Done:** Effortlessly discover, search, and stream high-quality movies, TV shows, and anime with auto-synced subtitles, ad-blocking, and seamless remote or keyboard/mouse controls without friction.

## Product Purpose

AetherFlix is a universal, self-hostable Smart TV and web streaming platform. It exists to provide an ad-free, high-fidelity, and distraction-free entertainment experience that feels like a $100M studio app while remaining open, accessible, and fast. Success means users can jump into content in under 5 seconds with zero cognitive fatigue.

## Brand Personality

- **3-Word Personality:** Cinematic, Restrained, Effortless.
- **Voice & Tone:** Confident, unobtrusive, premium, and focused. The UI acts as a silent gallery frame that elevates the cinematic artwork rather than drawing attention to itself.
- **Emotional Goals:** Immersion, calm comfort, and delight without visual overstimulation.

## Anti-references

- **AI Slop Clichés:** Decorative frosted glassmorphism everywhere, arbitrary glowing rainbow gradients, and generic floating shapes.
- **Illegible Dark UI:** Muted dark-gray-on-dark-gray text with poor contrast (<4.5:1) that strains the eyes in dark home theaters.
- **Sluggish Interaction:** Long bounce animations, slow page transitions, or layout shifts that frustrate users trying to browse quickly.
- **Desktop/TV Exclusivity:** Rigid layouts that break on narrow mobile viewports or clumsy hover states that fail on Smart TV D-pads and touchscreens.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is King, UI is the Canvas:** Backdrops, movie posters, and typography should dominate; chrome and controls must recede until summoned.
2. **Immediate Responsiveness:** Every click, keypress, and remote arrow input must provide instant, tactile feedback under 150ms.
3. **Flawless 10-Foot & 1-Foot Parity:** The interface must feel natural whether navigated with a high-precision mouse, touch swipe, or an Android TV remote control.
4. **Legibility in the Dark:** Deep OLED blacks paired with high-contrast, carefully tuned typographic scales ensure readability in all ambient lighting conditions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target Standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all UI text and interactive components.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Minimum 4.5:1 for body and secondary text against dark backgrounds; minimum 3:1 for large display titles and interactive focus indicators.
- **Reduced Motion:** Strict respect for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, replacing zoom/slide effects with swift cross-fades or immediate state updates.
- **Full Keyboard & D-Pad Navigation:** Unbroken tab navigation, explicit focus rings, and designated TV remote control keys (Arrow keys, Enter, Back/Escape).
